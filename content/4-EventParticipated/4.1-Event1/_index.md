# Cloud Architect Event

## Event information

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Event** | Cloud Architect Event |
| **Time** | 09:00–12:00, 20 June 2026 |
| **Location** | 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City |
| **My role** | Participant and team member answering the questions |
| **Topics** | AWS Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect Associate, and Solutions Architect Professional knowledge |

## Purpose

I joined the Cloud Architect Event to reinforce my AWS knowledge through an activity that combined learning and friendly competition. Instead of studying alone, I was able to discuss questions with my teammates, observe different reasoning approaches, and receive explanations for topics that were not yet clear to me.

The programme ran from 09:00 to 12:00 on the 26th floor of Bitexco Tower. Eight teams discussed and answered questions ranging from AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner fundamentals to Solutions Architect Associate design knowledge and more complex scenarios inspired by the Solutions Architect Professional level.

## Key content

The Cloud Practitioner questions reviewed cloud fundamentals, the shared responsibility model, AWS service categories, security, and cost management. The SAA- and SAP-oriented questions required participants to balance availability, scalability, durability, performance, security, and cost rather than merely recall service names.

![AWS architecture question](/images/4-EventParticipated/cloud-architect-event/aws-question.jpg "An Elastic Load Balancer question shown during the challenge")

*An AWS architecture question displayed for the teams to analyze.*

## Main activities

- Analyzed questions and agreed on answers with the team within a limited time.
- Reviewed the shared responsibility model, high availability, scalability, security, and cost optimization.
- Compared common AWS services and their appropriate use cases.
- Practiced architecture questions that required balancing several requirements.
- Listened to answer explanations and learned why one option was more suitable than the alternatives.
- Shared techniques for identifying keywords, eliminating incorrect answers, and managing time.

![Team discussion](/images/4-EventParticipated/cloud-architect-event/team-discussion.jpg "Team members discussing and selecting an answer")

*Team members compared their reasoning and agreed on an answer within the time limit.*

## Knowledge and skills gained

The Cloud Practitioner questions helped me organize foundational knowledge about AWS services, security, pricing, and cloud operations. The SAA questions encouraged me to consider resilience, performance, security, and cost when selecting an architecture. The SAP-oriented questions demonstrated that real-world solutions often require coordinated decisions across migration, multi-account governance, connectivity, and long-term operations.

The team format also improved my communication and problem-solving skills. With limited time, each member had to explain an opinion concisely, listen to alternatives, and share responsibility for the final answer.

## Application after the event

- Review the Cloud Practitioner domains that still require improvement.
- Identify architecture requirements and keywords before evaluating answers.
- Apply security, availability, and cost criteria when designing CampusMeet on AWS.
- Record incorrect answers and investigate their causes instead of memorizing solutions.
- Continue using team discussion for technical problems with multiple valid options.

## Result and reflection

My team **did not win an award**, but we did not treat the result as a disappointment. We remained positive until the end, reviewed incorrect answers together, and noted the areas that required further study.

For me, the most valuable outcome was the opportunity to learn in a relaxed and interactive environment with immediate explanations. Incorrect answers became useful learning moments because they exposed knowledge gaps and clarified the reasoning behind the correct solution.

The event motivated me to continue studying AWS, consolidate Cloud Practitioner fundamentals, and gradually develop architectural thinking at the SAA and SAP levels. It was also a positive teamwork experience: even without a prize, the team stayed enthusiastic and regarded learning as the most meaningful result.
